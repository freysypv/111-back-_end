Earth Day

Desert

The hot deserts can exceed 58°C (131°F) during the day but drop rapidly at night due to a lack of humidity. Polar deserts, like Antarctica, remain below freezing. The Sahara in Africa or the Arabian Desert are examples of hot deserts.

Cold Desert

Extremely cold, with snow or ice, like the Antarctic Desert (the largest desert in the world).

Forest

Forests cover 31% of the world's land area, providing essential habitat for 80% of land species, purifying air and water, and storing massive amounts of carbon.
